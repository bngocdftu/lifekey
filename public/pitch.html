2The constraint

Whoever runs the server can read every record.

Today

  • The server and its admins hold the keys
  • One breach or one insider exposes every patient
  • "Break the glass" is a checkbox an admin can bypass
  • So hospitals keep detailed records locked away, even in an emergency

What we need

  • Access for one doctor, one record, a short time
  • No single admin, server or AI can grant it alone
  • A leaked grant or ciphertext is useless to anyone else
  • The patient can see who opened what
3The prototype and TideCloak's role

Every record is encrypted on its own. TideCloak decides who may open it.
